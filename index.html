        <h3>核心定义</h3>
        <p>存在补丁主义（EP）是一个从三个不可消除的物理公设出发构建的动力学存在论框架。它探讨了局部偶然性如何通过补丁机制转化为宏观必然性。</p>
    </div>
    <div class="en">
        <h3>Core Definition</h3>
        <p>Existential Patchism (EP) is a dynamical ontological framework constructed from three ineradicable physical postulates. It explores how local contingency is transformed into macroscopic necessity through patching mechanisms.</p>
    </div>
</section>

<section>
    <h3>三个物理公设 | Three Physical Postulates</h3>
    
    <div class="bilingual">
        <div class="zh">
            <p><strong>1. 信息延迟：</strong> 光速极限迫使任何系统在信息缺失中行动，这是偶然性的物理根源。</p>
            <p><strong>2. 本体延迟：</strong> 系统必须持续支付代谢代价（热力学第二定律）以对抗解体。</p>
            <p><strong>3. 补丁机制：</strong> 系统通过动态生成的结构或关系（补丁）来吸收扰动，维持稳定。</p>
        </div>
        <div class="en">
            <p><strong>1. Information Delay:</strong> The limit of light speed forces any system to act in the absence of information, the physical root of contingency.</p>
            <p><strong>2. Ontological Delay:</strong> Systems must continuously pay metabolic costs (2nd Law of Thermodynamics) to resist disintegration.</p>
            <p><strong>3. Patching Mechanism:</strong> Systems maintain stability by absorbing perturbations through dynamically generated structures or relationships (Patches).</p>
        </div>
    </div>
</section>
